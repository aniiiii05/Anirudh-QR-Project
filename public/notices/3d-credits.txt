3D WORKSPACE — CREDITS AND PROVENANCE
=====================================

What it is
----------
"A little room for ideas" is an imaginary workspace. It is not a picture of
Anirudh Sharma's real home or office.

Everything you see in it — the room, desk, monitor and sample chart, keyboard,
mouse, mug, lamp, idea board and notes, notebook, stool, pouf, plant, shelf
and books, rug, window and the building blocks — is original procedural
geometry written for this project (src/card/three/roomScene.ts). There are no
downloaded 3D models, textures, sounds or fonts in the scene. The chart is
sample decoration, not real data.

The still image shown before "Explore in 3D" is a render of that same scene
from the same camera. It is regenerated with:

    node scripts/render-poster.mjs

which writes public/workspace-room.webp and public/workspace-room.png.
