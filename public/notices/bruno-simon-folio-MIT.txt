What was adapted
----------------
Only three small numeric helpers, from
  sources/Game/utilities/maths.js  (at the commit above)
were adapted for this project (TypeScript types added; the arithmetic is unchanged):

  - clamp(input, min, max)
  - lerp(start, end, ratio)
  - smoothstep(value, min, max)

They live in src/card/three/maths.ts and are used for the camera transition
easing and bounds of the 3D workspace. Nothing else from folio-2025 is used:
no game, physics, vehicle, world, sound, shader, model, texture, font or
portfolio content.
